2. Age Requirements

Monster Outlaws – Inquisition Breakout is intended for players aged 13 and older.

When first launching the Game, players are asked to select their age range: (Under 13, 13–15, 16–17, 18 or older)

The selected age range is stored locally on the player's device and is used to determine access to the Game and apply appropriate advertising and privacy protections.

Players who indicate they are under 13 are not permitted to access the Game.

We do not require players to provide their exact date of birth.

The Game is not directed toward children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information directly from children under 13.

Additional advertising and privacy protections may apply to teenage players depending on their age, location, and applicable laws.

Players under the age of majority should obtain permission from a parent or legal guardian before making purchases.

3. Locally Stored Game Information

The Game stores the following information locally on your device:

Character levels and unlocked characters.

Inventory, equipment, and upgrades.

5. Google AdMob Advertising

The Game uses Google AdMob to provide advertisements.

The Game includes two types of advertisements:

Mission-End Advertisements

Advertisements may appear after completing missions.

Players can permanently disable these advertisements through the optional Remove Mission Ads Forever purchase.

Optional Rewarded Advertisements

Players may voluntarily watch eligible rewarded advertisements to receive 100 virtual Gold.

Rewarded advertisements remain available even after purchasing mission-ad removal.

Watching rewarded advertisements is optional and is not required to continue playing.

Google and its advertising partners may process information including:

IP addresses and approximate location inferred from them.

Advertising and device identifiers.

Advertising interactions.

Device and operating system information.

Diagnostic and performance information.

Advertising may be personalized or non-personalized, depending on applicable laws, consent, age-related restrictions, and configuration.

6. In-App Purchases

The Game offers optional in-app purchases through supported app store payment systems and RevenueCat.

Remove Mission Ads Forever — $2.99 USD

This one-time, non-consumable purchase permanently removes mission-end advertisements.

Optional rewarded advertisements remain available for players who wish to earn additional Gold.

The purchase can be restored through supported purchase-restoration functionality.

Virtual Gold Purchases

Players may purchase virtual Gold using real money.

Virtual Gold:

Is used only within the Game.

Has no real-world monetary value.

Cannot be exchanged for cash.

Cannot be transferred or sold for real-world currency.

Is subject to the Game's purchase and usage rules.

Purchased Gold is added to the current campaign.

Consumable Gold purchases are not restorable through the standard Restore Purchases feature.

Loading an older save may restore the Gold balance recorded in that save.

Payment Processing

In-app purchases are processed through the applicable app store and RevenueCat.

We do not directly receive or store complete payment card information.
